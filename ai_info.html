Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Applications of AI

  • Chatbots and Virtual Assistants
  • Self-Driving Cars
  • Healthcare and Disease Detection
  • Recommendation Systems
  • Computer Vision
  • Natural Language Processing

Popular AI Technologies

Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing are some important areas of Artificial Intelligence.
